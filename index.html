Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Can I see my website before it goes live?

Yes. Our planned process includes a private preview for your review and approval before final publication.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. We agree on revision rounds in your proposal so you know what is included.

Can you add bookings, online payments or a store?

Tell us which features you need. We will confirm feasibility, third-party costs and a custom quote before starting.

How much will my website cost?

Projects are quoted based on pages, design and features. Share your budget in the website planner to receive a suitable recommendation. A recommendation is not a final price.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Responsive layouts are part of our design approach. Your project will be checked on mobile-sized screens before handover.

Does the planning assistant send my answers automatically?

Not yet. The current assistant helps you prepare a summary to copy and share. We will clearly announce when secure online submission is available.
